How I work

Good judgment for a constant flow of requests.

At Cisco I took the StandOut strengths assessment, and my top two roles came back as Provider and Advisor. Together, they describe my greatest value to a team as “the ability to bring good judgment to a constant flow of requests.” It’s the most accurate description of me at work I’ve ever read, because that constant flow is exactly where I’m happiest.

At Duo my messages were full of Terraform questions, guardrail questions, and engineers stuck on something new, and honestly, I loved it. The report puts it better than I can: “your genuine desire to help is your advantage.” So if you bring me your problem, you’re not interrupting me. You’re handing me my favorite part of the job.

Role 1

Provider

“Providers sense other people’s feelings, and they feel compelled to recognize these feelings, give them a voice, and act on them.”

In practice: I notice when a teammate is stuck before they say so. I remember what people are working on and what matters to them, and I make sure the person who needs help actually gets it. People trust me quickly because I don’t judge.

Role 2

Advisor

“Advisors are practical, concrete thinkers who are at their most powerful when reacting to and solving other people’s problems.”

In practice: Bring me a messy problem and I’ll break it into pieces, ask a lot of questions, and come back with the next concrete step. The question I always end up asking is “what’s really going to work?”
